## About this project

**AquaLink** is a community water supply business concept set in Muraleni village, Limpopo. I worked on it with a five-person student team as the **Project Manager**, planning the work in Agile sprints and tracking it on Trello.

Each page below has two parts: the **Evidence** (the artefact itself) and my **Reflection** on what it taught me, written with the STAR method.
